Investigating the Impact of Technology Use on Academic Performance among College Students

A literature-based investigation by first-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering students at Batangas State University (TNEU) – Alangilan Campus, exploring how digital devices, screen-time habits, and educational technologies shape academic performance in a hybrid learning era.
