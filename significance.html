info Abstract Impact Statement

This study aims to understand how technology use affects the academic performance of first-year Computer Engineering students at Batangas State University – Alangilan Campus. The findings may provide valuable insights into the benefits and challenges of technology in education and may benefit the following individuals and groups:

Students

First-Year Computer Engineering

This study may benefit first-year Computer Engineering students by helping them understand how their use of technology affects their academic performance. It may encourage them to develop responsible and effective technology habits, such as using digital tools for research, studying, communication, and completing academic tasks. Furthermore, it may help them recognize potential distractions, including excessive social media use and non-academic activities, that could negatively affect their learning and productivity.

Core Outcome Habitual Self-Regulation

Teachers and Educators

Academic Instruction & Curricula

The findings of this study may help teachers and educators understand how technology influences students' learning habits, classroom participation, and academic performance. Through the results, they may identify effective ways to integrate digital tools and educational platforms into their teaching strategies. It may also help them develop appropriate approaches to guide students in using technology productively while minimizing its possible negative effects on learning.

Core Outcome Digital Tool Optimization
