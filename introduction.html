Background of the Study

In the 21st century, students rely heavily on technology for their academic work, as many institutions facing facility constraints have shifted from traditional classroom setups to hybrid learning models. This reality directly applies to Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (CpE) students at Batangas State University (TNEU) – Alangilan Campus, whose program requires constant exposure to specialized software, hardware, and digital tools. In line with this, a correlation study by Siega (2025) explores the relationship between computer technology usage and the academic performance of Computer Engineering students, demonstrating that technology exerts a significant impact on their academic journey.

Background of Technology in Higher Education

Wilen-Daugenti (2008)

The educational technology has evolved from traditional classroom tools to advanced digital technology, shifting universities from traditional lectures to modern and flexible learning. As Wilen-Daugenti (2008) highlighted the increasing use of digital devices and online learning platforms. This change allows students to access information and collaborate anytime anywhere.

Technology Use Among Filipino College Students

Gayrama (2025)

According to Gayrama (2025), Filipino college students increasingly rely on digital devices, internet access and online learning platforms to manage their tasks and regulate their study in a blended and hybrid environment. As it allows them to adapt to modern educational demands and stay connected with their peers and instructors despite physical distance.

Engineering Hardware & SBCs YEAR: 2022

Technology in Computer Engineering Education

Ariza and Baez (2022)

According to Ariza and Baez (2022), single board computers (SBCs) enhance engineering education by enabling students to master complex concepts through hands-on experiences in laboratories, robotics etc. Consequently, integrating these low-cost tools significantly improves students grades, technical skills, and overall academic performance.

Dual-Edge Cognitive Tax YEAR: 2025

Relationship Between Technology Use and Academic Performance

OECD (2025)

According to the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD,2025), digital technologies enhance students' academic performance by exposing them to the new educational resources, educational resources, and personalized learning efficiency. However, the study emphasizes that the access to technology alone does not guarantee higher grades, as unregulated device use frequently triggers classroom distractions, weak study habits, and poor time management that can affect students' academic performance.

OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY

This study aims to investigate the impact of technology use on the academic performance of college students through existing literature, published studies, and relevant research findings.

01 Patterns & Purposes

To determine the common patterns and purposes of technology use among college students.

schedule Usage frequency, modality & screen duration
02 Positive Effects

To identify the perceived positive effects of technology use on students’ academic activities.

trending_up Resource availability, peer collaboration & labs
03 Negative Effects

To examine the perceived negative effects of technology use on students’ academic activities.

warning Classroom multitasking, sleep debt & delay
04 Performance Extent

To assess the perceived extent of the influence of technology use on the academic performance of college students.

analytics Empirical GWA correlation & task completion
